ACME CORPORATION — HR POLICY DOCUMENT
Version 3.2 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025
========================================

SECTION 1: GENERAL PROVISIONS

1.1 Purpose
This document outlines the human resources policies applicable to all full-time and part-time employees of Acme Corporation. These policies are designed to ensure a fair, consistent, and productive work environment.

1.2 Scope
These policies apply to all employees across all departments and locations of Acme Corporation, unless explicitly stated otherwise.

1.3 Amendments
The HR department reserves the right to amend these policies with 30 days written notice to all employees. Amendments will be communicated via email and posted on the internal employee portal.

SECTION 2: WORKING HOURS AND ATTENDANCE

2.1 Standard Working Hours
The standard work week consists of 40 hours, typically Monday through Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM with a one-hour lunch break. Flexible working hours may be arranged with manager approval.

2.2 Attendance Policy
All employees are expected to maintain regular attendance. Employees must notify their immediate supervisor at least one hour before their scheduled start time if they will be absent or late. Three consecutive unnotified absences may result in disciplinary action.

2.3 Overtime
Overtime work requires prior written approval from the department head. Overtime hours are compensated at 1.5x the regular hourly rate for weekdays and 2x for weekends and public holidays.

SECTION 3: COMPENSATION AND BENEFITS

3.1 Salary Structure
Salaries are determined based on job grade, experience, and market benchmarks. Annual salary reviews are conducted in April each year.

3.2 Performance Bonuses
Eligible employees may receive performance bonuses based on individual and company performance metrics. Bonuses are typically disbursed in March following the performance year.

3.3 Health Insurance
All full-time employees are enrolled in the company group health insurance plan, which covers the employee, spouse, and up to two dependent children. Coverage includes hospitalization, outpatient treatment, dental, and vision care up to the policy limits.

3.4 Provident Fund
The company contributes 12% of the employee's basic salary to the provident fund. Employees may voluntarily increase their contribution up to an additional 12%.

SECTION 4: LEAVE POLICY

4.1 Annual Leave (Earned Leave)
Full-time employees are entitled to 18 days of annual leave per calendar year. Leave is accrued at the rate of 1.5 days per month. Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 30 days.

4.2 Casual Leave
Employees get 12 casual leaves per year. Casual leave cannot be carried forward to the next year and expires on December 31st. A maximum of 3 consecutive casual leave days can be taken at a time.

4.3 Sick Leave
Employees are entitled to 10 days of sick leave per year. Sick leave exceeding 3 consecutive days requires a medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner. Unused sick leave cannot be encashed or carried forward.

4.4 Maternity Leave
Female employees are entitled to 26 weeks of paid maternity leave for the first two children and 12 weeks for subsequent children. Maternity leave can be availed up to 8 weeks before the expected delivery date.

4.5 Paternity Leave
Male employees are entitled to 10 days of paid paternity leave within 6 months of the birth of their child.

4.6 Bereavement Leave
Employees are granted up to 5 days of paid bereavement leave in case of death of an immediate family member (spouse, parent, child, or sibling).

4.7 Public Holidays
Acme Corporation observes 12 public holidays per year. The list of public holidays is published at the beginning of each calendar year.

4.8 Leave Application Process
All leave requests must be submitted through the HR portal at least 3 working days in advance for planned leave. Emergency leave requests should be communicated to the immediate supervisor as soon as possible.

SECTION 5: CODE OF CONDUCT

5.1 Professional Behavior
All employees are expected to maintain professional conduct at all times. This includes treating colleagues, clients, and partners with respect and dignity.

5.2 Anti-Harassment Policy
Acme Corporation has zero tolerance for harassment of any kind, including sexual harassment, bullying, and discrimination. Any incidents must be reported to the HR department or the Internal Complaints Committee (ICC).

5.3 Conflict of Interest
Employees must avoid situations where personal interests conflict with company interests. Any potential conflicts must be disclosed to the HR department in writing.

5.4 Confidentiality
Employees must maintain confidentiality of all proprietary information, trade secrets, and client data both during and after employment. Violation of confidentiality may result in termination and legal action.

5.5 Social Media Policy
Employees should exercise discretion when using social media. Posting confidential company information, defamatory content about the company or colleagues, or content that could damage the company's reputation is strictly prohibited.

SECTION 6: DISCIPLINARY PROCEDURES

6.1 Warning System
The disciplinary process follows a progressive approach: verbal warning, written warning, final written warning, and termination. Severe misconduct may result in immediate termination.

6.2 Grievance Redressal
Employees who wish to raise a grievance should first approach their immediate supervisor. If unresolved, the grievance can be escalated to the HR department and subsequently to the Grievance Committee.

SECTION 7: SEPARATION

7.1 Notice Period
The standard notice period is 30 days for regular employees and 90 days for senior management (Grade L5 and above). The notice period may be waived at the company's discretion with payment in lieu of notice.

7.2 Exit Process
Departing employees must complete the exit checklist, which includes returning company property, knowledge transfer, and an exit interview with HR.

7.3 Full and Final Settlement
The full and final settlement will be processed within 45 days of the last working day, subject to completion of the exit process and clearance from all departments.
