ACME CORPORATION — EMPLOYEE HANDBOOK
Version 4.0 | Effective Date: January 1, 2025
===============================================

WELCOME TO ACME CORPORATION

Welcome to Acme Corporation! We are delighted to have you as part of our team. This handbook is designed to help you understand our culture, values, and the policies that guide our workplace. Please read it thoroughly and reach out to HR if you have any questions.

SECTION 1: ABOUT ACME CORPORATION

1.1 Company Overview
Acme Corporation was founded in 2005 and has grown to become a leading provider of enterprise technology solutions. With offices in Bangalore, Mumbai, Delhi, and Hyderabad, we serve over 500 clients across 30 countries.

1.2 Mission Statement
To empower businesses through innovative technology solutions that drive efficiency, growth, and digital transformation.

1.3 Core Values
- Innovation: We embrace creativity and continuously seek better solutions
- Integrity: We conduct business with honesty and transparency
- Collaboration: We believe in the power of teamwork
- Excellence: We strive for the highest quality in everything we do
- Customer Focus: Our clients' success is our success

SECTION 2: ONBOARDING PROCESS

2.1 First Day Checklist
On your first day, you will:
- Receive your employee ID card and access credentials
- Complete IT setup with the helpdesk team
- Attend a welcome orientation session
- Meet your team and reporting manager
- Receive your company laptop and equipment

2.2 Probation Period
New employees undergo a probation period of 6 months. During probation, performance will be reviewed at the 3-month and 6-month marks. Confirmation is subject to satisfactory performance.

2.3 Buddy Program
Every new employee is assigned a buddy — an experienced colleague who will help you navigate the organization during your first 90 days. Your buddy will introduce you to key stakeholders and help you settle in.

2.4 Training Requirements
Within the first 30 days, new employees must complete:
- Company orientation program (2 days)
- IT security awareness training
- Code of conduct acknowledgment
- Role-specific technical training (as assigned by the reporting manager)

SECTION 3: WORKPLACE POLICIES

3.1 Dress Code
Acme Corporation follows a business casual dress code from Monday to Thursday. Fridays are designated as casual dress days. Employees meeting external clients should dress in business formal attire.

3.2 Workspace
Employees are expected to maintain a clean and organized workspace. Hot-desking is available in select offices. Reserved desks can be booked through the facility management portal.

3.3 Meeting Room Etiquette
- Book meeting rooms in advance through the calendar system
- Release the room if you no longer need it
- Leave the room clean and organized
- Respect time — start and end meetings punctually

3.4 Cafeteria and Pantry
The company cafeteria provides subsidized meals during lunch hours (12:00 PM - 2:00 PM). Tea and coffee are available free of charge in all pantry areas throughout the day.

SECTION 4: PERFORMANCE MANAGEMENT

4.1 Goal Setting
At the beginning of each performance cycle (April), employees set goals in collaboration with their managers using the OKR (Objectives and Key Results) framework.

4.2 Performance Reviews
Performance reviews are conducted semi-annually:
- Mid-year review: October
- Annual review: March
Reviews include self-assessment, manager assessment, and 360-degree feedback for senior roles.

4.3 Performance Improvement Plan (PIP)
Employees whose performance is rated below expectations may be placed on a Performance Improvement Plan lasting 60-90 days. The PIP outlines specific improvement targets and support measures.

4.4 Promotions
Promotions are based on sustained performance, potential, and role availability. The annual promotion cycle occurs in April, following the performance review.

SECTION 5: LEARNING AND DEVELOPMENT

5.1 Training Budget
Each employee has an annual learning and development budget of INR 50,000 that can be used for courses, certifications, conferences, and books.

5.2 Internal Learning Platform
Acme offers an internal learning management system (LMS) with over 2,000 courses covering technical skills, leadership, communication, and project management.

5.3 Certification Reimbursement
The company reimburses 100% of certification exam fees for pre-approved certifications relevant to the employee's role. The reimbursement requires passing the certification exam.

5.4 Mentorship Program
Senior employees can volunteer as mentors. Mentees are matched based on career interests and development goals. The mentorship cycle runs for 6 months.

SECTION 6: EMPLOYEE BENEFITS

6.1 Employee Assistance Program (EAP)
The EAP provides confidential counseling and support services for personal and work-related issues. Services include mental health counseling, financial advisory, and legal consultation.

6.2 Wellness Program
The company offers a comprehensive wellness program including:
- Annual health check-ups
- Gym membership reimbursement (up to INR 2,000/month)
- Yoga and meditation sessions (weekly)
- Mental health awareness workshops

6.3 Employee Referral Program
Employees who refer successful candidates receive a referral bonus ranging from INR 25,000 to INR 1,00,000 depending on the role level.

6.4 Transportation
Company-provided cab service is available for employees working late shifts (after 8:00 PM) or early shifts (before 7:00 AM). Regular transport facilities vary by office location.
