ACME CORPORATION — REMOTE WORK POLICY
Version 1.5 | Effective Date: June 1, 2025
============================================

SECTION 1: OVERVIEW

1.1 Purpose
This policy establishes guidelines for remote work (Work From Home) arrangements at Acme Corporation. It aims to provide flexibility while maintaining productivity, collaboration, and security standards.

1.2 Applicability
This policy applies to all full-time employees who have completed their probation period. Contractors and interns are eligible for remote work only with explicit written approval from the department head.

SECTION 2: ELIGIBILITY AND APPROVAL

2.1 Eligibility Criteria
Employees are eligible for remote work if they:
- Have completed the 6-month probation period
- Have a performance rating of "Meets Expectations" or above in the last review cycle
- Are in a role that can be performed remotely (as determined by the department head)
- Have a suitable home workspace with reliable internet connectivity (minimum 50 Mbps)

2.2 Remote Work Models
Acme Corporation offers three remote work models:
- Hybrid (Default): 3 days in office, 2 days remote per week (Tuesday and Thursday are mandatory in-office days)
- Primarily Remote: Up to 4 days remote per week (requires VP approval)
- Fully Remote: 5 days remote (exceptional cases only, requires CHRO approval)

2.3 Approval Process
To request a remote work arrangement:
1. Discuss with your reporting manager
2. Submit a Remote Work Request form on the HR portal
3. Manager reviews and approves/denies within 5 business days
4. Approved arrangements are reviewed quarterly

SECTION 3: REMOTE WORK GUIDELINES

3.1 Work Hours
Remote employees must be available during core business hours (10:00 AM - 4:00 PM IST). Flexible start and end times are permitted outside core hours, provided the total work hours meet the 8-hour daily requirement.

3.2 Communication
- Employees must be reachable via Microsoft Teams during work hours
- Camera should be turned on during team meetings and client calls
- Daily stand-up meetings with the team are mandatory
- Response time for messages should not exceed 30 minutes during core hours

3.3 Workspace Requirements
Remote employees must ensure:
- A dedicated, quiet workspace free from distractions
- Ergonomic setup (the company provides a one-time INR 15,000 allowance for home office setup)
- Reliable internet connection (minimum 50 Mbps; the company reimburses up to INR 1,500/month for internet)
- Adequate lighting for video calls

3.4 Productivity Tracking
- Employees must update tasks on Jira/project management tools daily
- Weekly status reports must be submitted to the reporting manager by Friday 5:00 PM
- Managers may schedule periodic check-ins to discuss progress and address challenges

SECTION 4: EQUIPMENT AND SUPPORT

4.1 Company Equipment
Remote employees will be provided with:
- Laptop with required software pre-installed
- VPN access for secure connectivity
- Microsoft 365 license
- Access to IT helpdesk for remote support

4.2 Home Office Allowance
A one-time home office setup allowance of INR 15,000 is provided to purchase:
- Monitor
- Keyboard and mouse
- Headset
- Desk lamp
Receipts must be submitted within 30 days of purchase.

4.3 Internet Reimbursement
The company reimburses internet costs up to INR 1,500 per month upon submission of monthly bills.

SECTION 5: SECURITY AND COMPLIANCE

5.1 Data Security
All remote work must comply with the IT Security Policy. Specifically:
- Always use the company VPN when accessing internal systems
- Do not use public Wi-Fi without VPN protection
- Keep the laptop's screen locked when not in use
- Do not allow unauthorized persons to access company devices or data

5.2 Confidentiality
Remote employees must ensure phone calls and video meetings discussing confidential matters are conducted in private spaces where conversations cannot be overheard.

5.3 Physical Security
Company equipment must be stored securely when not in use. Report any lost or stolen equipment to the IT Security team within 1 hour.

SECTION 6: ATTENDANCE AND LEAVE

6.1 Remote Work vs. Leave
Working from home is not a substitute for leave. Employees who are unwell should apply for sick leave rather than attempting to work remotely. Remote work days should be productive work days.

6.2 In-Office Requirements
Certain activities require in-office presence:
- Team building events and workshops
- Client visits and presentations
- Performance review discussions
- Onboarding activities (first 2 weeks)
- Quarterly town halls

SECTION 7: TERMINATION OF REMOTE WORK ARRANGEMENT

7.1 Company-Initiated Changes
The company reserves the right to modify or terminate remote work arrangements with 2 weeks' notice based on business needs, performance concerns, or policy changes.

7.2 Employee-Initiated Changes
Employees wishing to change their remote work arrangement should submit a new request through the HR portal.
